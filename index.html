Gaming: The Musical · Production updates

Where Gaming and Theatre Meet

Side Quest Theatre Co. is an independent performing arts collective dedicated to developing original theatrical works rooted in contemporary digital and gaming culture. Staging our flagship Winter 2027 production, Gaming: The Musical, we bring together performers, designers, and audiences to reimagine live stagecraft for a new generation.
